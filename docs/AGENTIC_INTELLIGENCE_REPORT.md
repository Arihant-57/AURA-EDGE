# Agentic intelligence verification

The assistant uses local grammars for clock, timer and routine requests, a bounded typed tool registry for operations, and local Qwen for explanations. PostgreSQL and the simulated device service supply state. Simple addition, subtraction and multiplication of two recognized integers use a deterministic tool. A live Qwen answer gave 122 for seventeen times six. The calculator returned 102 on the same prompt.

| --- | --- | --- |
| English negation and hypothetical device questions selected DeviceControl | Explicit negation, including Hindi and Kannada forms, is intercepted. Hypothetical questions use the conversational path. | Six independent routing cases and live device-history comparisons. |
| A compound AC and reminder command created only a reminder | Split supported instructions before interpreting the whole utterance as a reminder. | A plan could contain seven calls or continue after a failed step | Limit to four calls, preflight ordinary device targets and capability ranges, then stop on failure. Preserve completed sub-results and verified context. | Router boundary, invalid later temperature, ambiguous later device, compound briefing and pending-task checks. Previously finished mutations remain in the audit. |
| A routine with failed, partial or unmet-condition results produced a success announcement | Ground spoken feedback in the actual step results. Include the completed count on partial failure. Mark a scheduled skipped routine as an unsuccessful execution with its reason. | Separate regression cases for all three voice outcomes. |
| A conversational model streamed an unverified claim into the dashboard | Validate the complete answer before returning its text to the desktop, browser and speech pipeline. Record first validated text separately from first model token. | Controlled WebSocket reproduction for a claim split across tokens. Free-form multilingual claim detection remains a pattern-based safeguard. |
| “What can you do?” used a static phrase | Assemble the localized answer per wired service, including optional household services. Keep internal class names out of speech. | English, Hindi and Kannada service-inventory checks and live English Help. Service presence and backing database health are separate. |

The factual harness checks expected concepts, language and route. It includes a contextual follow-up, Hindi, Kannada, offline weather, arithmetic and Help. Its public questions and full answers can be reviewed individually. These small samples do not characterize general world knowledge, all phrasings or adversarial prompts.
